Customs Border Protection Process Guide

Inspection Information Collection

The inspection follows a structured 14-step process:

  1. Inspection ID
  2. Inspection Date
  3. Port of Entry
  4. Inspection Type
  5. Individual or Company
  6. Citizenship or Origin
  7. Purpose of Entry
  8. Documents Presented
  9. Declarations Made
  10. Items Inspected
  11. Violations Found
  12. Enforcement Action
  13. Officer Remarks
  14. Case Status

Tips for Best Results

  • Provide detailed and accurate information
  • Use specific dates and times when possible
  • Include all relevant details about documents
  • Be thorough in describing inspection procedures
