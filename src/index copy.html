Investigation Process Guide

Case Information Collection

The investigation follows a structured 14-step process:

  1. Case ID
  2. Date of Crime
  3. Time of Crime
  4. Location
  5. Victim Name
  6. Victim Age
  7. Victim Gender
  8. Cause of Death
  9. Weapon Used
  10. Crime Scene Description
  11. Witnesses
  12. Evidence Found
  13. Suspects
  14. Additional Notes

Tips for Best Results

  • Provide detailed and accurate information
  • Use specific dates and times when possible
  • Include all relevant details about evidence
  • Be thorough in witness descriptions
