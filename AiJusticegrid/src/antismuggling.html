Anti Smuggling Investigation Process Guide

Case Information Collection

The investigation follows a structured 14-step process:

  1. Case ID
  2. Date of Incident
  3. Port of Entry
  4. Type of Smuggling
  5. Detection Method
  6. Suspect Information
  7. Travel Document Review
  8. Vehicle or Conveyance
  9. Contraband Description
  10. Interview Summary
  11. Surveillance or Intel
  12. Seizure Details
  13. Agency Coordination
  14. Case Status

Tips for Best Results

  • Provide detailed and accurate information
  • Use specific dates and times when possible
  • Include all relevant details about contraband
  • Be thorough in describing detection methods
